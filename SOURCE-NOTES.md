## Editorial decisions

- The site’s English narrative is new editorial synthesis. It is not presented as a direct quotation or a documented personal reflection by Zihan.
- The environment PDF is individually attributed, but both spreadsheets describe collaborative research and an assistant role. The site preserves both facts, rather than labeling this an independent project.
- The workforce PDF alone does not identify Zihan; the activity list explicitly supports her research-assistant role, data analysis, and contribution to model building. The site identifies the report as institutional team work.
- The mini-program paper has an individual byline and discusses team practical work. The activity list describes Zihan as researcher and main developer. Pilot feedback is not presented as demonstrated clinical effectiveness. The activity list’s 300+ users claim is not used as an evaluated outcome.
- Documentary responsibilities are supported by Activity List row 8: scriptwriting, initiative founding and organization. The site does not invent director credit, film duration, production date, awards, interview subjects, or viewer impact. The user subsequently supplied https://youtu.be/SJVlUEBVMM4 and identified it as her documentary. The Documentary section now embeds that video, with a direct YouTube fallback link.
- Gender, invisible labor, and links among the research projects are interpretive framing requested by the user. A paid-care workforce, unpaid grandchild care, and a son's family-care story remain distinct contexts. The film does not validate the regression findings.
- Factors chart: coefficients reproduced exactly as β in Table 1; association language replaces causal language in the source. “Caregiving burden” is narrowed to grandchild-care burden. No raw dataset was supplied. No confidence intervals are invented. Coefficients are not described as a single jointly estimated model or ranked causal effects.
- Environment chart uses a 0–100% baseline and specifies top-two selection; it does not depict the categories as a partition of respondents.
- The two sample sizes are reported separately and never added to claim a unique-person reach. Overlap between the samples is unknown.
- Workforce results are conditional projections. The 94.48% figure concerns the report’s in-service caregiver workforce, not all Shanghai caregivers or unpaid family care.
- HiMCM filename and project spreadsheet indicate 2025, while the PDF cover says 2026. No year or award is asserted. Team authorship is supported by the activity list; individual algorithm ownership is not claimed.
- School name and Audrey preferred name are supported by the uploaded papers. Unrelated activities and honors are omitted to maintain a focused research portfolio.
- The generic 404 page uses relative links; for an invalid deeply nested path on a project-site deployment, use the browser address bar to return to the published site root.
